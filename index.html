Orbit Hop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Orbit Hop (package com.omarmansy.orbithop), developed by SanabelApps ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. Orbit Hop does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data. The game has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not request internet access.

Ratings

The only third-party component is Google Play's in-app review library. When you choose to rate the game, it shows Google Play's rating window; any rating or review you submit is sent by the Play Store app directly to Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive it from the game.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, collected stars, unlocked skins and sound setting are saved locally on your device so your progress is kept between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

The app uses only the vibration permission, to give short haptic feedback during play.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Email omarmansy96@gmail.com.
