Madar Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Madar (package com.sanabelapps.madar), developed by SanabelApps ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive or share any personal information or usage data. The game has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not request internet access.

Data saved on your device

To keep your progress between sessions, the game saves your best scores, collected stars, unlocked skins, mission progress and settings (sound and language) on your device only. We have no access to this data. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include it in your device backup, which is handled by Google. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it from your device.

Ratings

The only third-party component is Google Play's in-app review library. When you choose to rate the game, it shows Google Play's rating window. Any rating or review you submit goes directly to Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; the game never receives it.

Permissions

The game uses only the vibration permission, for short haptic feedback during play.

Children's privacy

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Email support@sanabelapps.com.
